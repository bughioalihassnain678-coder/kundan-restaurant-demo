About Kundan Restaurant

Where Warm Hospitality Meets Flame-Grilled Flavor

Located on Khanpur Road in Shikarpur, Kundan Restaurant brings together the rich aroma of live charcoal barbecue and the comfort of welcoming family dining.

From generous shared platters and tender marinated boti skewers to crisp golden snacks, every dish on our menu is prepared to celebrate bold seasoning and satisfying portions. Whether you are gathering for an evening meal, picking up a quick takeout order, or enjoying open-air seating, our focus remains simple: memorable food worth sharing.
